Why import VAT lagged import growth

Total imports rose 17.34% but VAT on imports grew only 8.67% (shortfall vs target ~433–482 bn depending on series). Nearly three-quarters of the import increase was non-VATable.

PAYE shortfall & employment register

Collections UGX 5,815.75 bn (97.1% of target); shortfall ~175 bn. Growth +8.0%.

  • 3,819 taxpayers with prior PAYE payments recorded zero in FY25/26.
  • 11,538 taxpayers reported declines in employee numbers.
  • Overall employees on record still rose: 984,585 → 1,044,955 (+60,370).

Largest PAYE declines by sector: extraterritorial organisations (–19.2%), human health (–8.7%), public administration (–4.4%).

Other parliamentary context

Gold sector

Export levy collections UGX 50.40 bn (+42.2%). Relative to export earnings of ~US$ 8.91 bn the levy remains modest.

Tax expenditure

Projected FY 2025/26 cost UGX 5,406.97 bn (2.17% of GDP). MoFPED publishes the annual assessment; new-measure costing still pending final validation.

Fiscal deficit & Charter

Deficit of 6.6% of GDP in the final Charter year (vs 3.0% ceiling) attributed to shocks, elections and infrastructure. Path to 1.5% by FY 2030/31 relies on oil, DRMS II and expenditure prioritisation.

Debt stock vs deficit

Public debt rose UGX 19.28 Tn while deficit was 14.99 Tn; difference linked to disbursement timing, exchange rates and prior-year carry-overs. Errors & omissions 851 bn reflect cross-source timing differences.
